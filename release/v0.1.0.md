## SRT Studio for Windows

Download and extract **SRT-Studio-0.1.0-Windows.zip**. Run **Install-SRT-Studio.cmd**, choose NVIDIA or CPU, and prepare Turbo or Small for offline use. Then open **Start-SRT-Studio.cmd**.

- Batch transcription with CUDA or CPU, live progress, and SRT, VTT, and TXT export.
- An offline preparation launcher downloads and verifies the selected Whisper model before you disconnect.
- The workbench opens on localhost. Your audio stays on your PC.

Requires 64-bit Python and FFmpeg. The ZIP does not include dependencies or model files. Internet is needed for the first setup and each new model.

Prefer no install? [Open the browser edition](https://clankclankclankclank.github.io/SRTstudio/). Accurate mode is the default and produces word-timed captions.
